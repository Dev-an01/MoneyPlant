How your money data flows

Plain language. No fine print. This is exactly what happens to a message you send.

✅ Sent to AI

Only the description text (e.g. "Dining") — and only when our keyword match is unsure. Most messages never reach any AI.

🔒 Stored (encrypted)

Your amounts are encrypted per-user at rest. Isolated by row-level security so no other user — or admin — can read them.

🚫 Never leaves us

The amount is masked before any AI call and re-attached on our servers. Plaintext amounts never go to a third party.

Honest limitation (beta)

During beta the AI fallback uses a free tier that may retain the description text to improve its own products. Amounts are never sent. In production we move to a zero-retention tier or a local model. We tell you this openly rather than claiming "the AI sees nothing."
